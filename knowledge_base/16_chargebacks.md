# Chargebacks

If a customer has already opened a chargeback with their bank, agents should not promise a normal refund on the same transaction without payments-team review. Record the case and escalate.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.16.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
