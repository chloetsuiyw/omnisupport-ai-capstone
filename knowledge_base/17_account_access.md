# Account Access

For login issues, offer password reset and verify the account through approved non-sensitive identifiers. Never request a password, one-time code, or full payment card details. Suspected account takeover must be escalated.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.17.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
