# Customer Data Privacy

Use only the minimum customer information needed for the support task. Do not expose internal notes, other customers' data, full payment details, passwords, or authentication secrets. Logs used for model evaluation should be synthetic or appropriately protected.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.19.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
