# Support Communication Style

Use concise, respectful language. Avoid blaming customers. Separate confirmed facts from next steps. Do not claim that a refund, replacement, or cancellation has occurred unless the relevant tool confirms it.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.33.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
