# Support Priority

Urgent priority is reserved for safety, active account compromise, essential-goods disruption, or severe repeated service failure. High priority covers material financial or operational impact. Ordinary status questions are generally medium or low.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.28.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
