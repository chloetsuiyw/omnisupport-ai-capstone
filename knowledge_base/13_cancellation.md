# Order Cancellation

Orders can usually be cancelled before warehouse dispatch. Once dispatch is confirmed, the customer should use the returns process instead. Cancellation requests for marketplace items may require seller confirmation.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.13.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
