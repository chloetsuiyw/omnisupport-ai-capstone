# Failed Payments

If checkout reports a failed payment, first confirm whether an order was actually created. A pending bank authorisation is not the same as a captured payment. Do not ask customers to send full card numbers through chat or email.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.14.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
