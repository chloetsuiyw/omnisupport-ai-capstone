# Damaged on Arrival

Customers should report visible damage as soon as practical. A photo is helpful but not mandatory when damage is obvious from the description. For high-value electronics, an image may be requested before a replacement or refund is approved. Safety-critical damage should be escalated.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.05.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
