# Loyalty Points

Points normally post within 72 hours after an eligible order is marked completed. Returned items can cause corresponding points to be removed. Manual points adjustments above 5,000 points require supervisor approval.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.20.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
