# Fashion Returns

Fashion items follow the 30-day standard window. Hygiene seals on certain garments must remain intact. Footwear should be tried on indoors. Items that are clearly worn beyond inspection may be rejected or partially refunded.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.03.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
