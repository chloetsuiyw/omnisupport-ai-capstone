# Duplicate Charge

Possible duplicate charges should be checked against order and payment references. Where two captured payments are confirmed for one order, escalate to payments support. Pending authorisations can disappear without a refund transaction.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.15.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
