# Refund Calculator Tool

The refund calculator computes a proposed amount from order value, item condition, and approved policy rules. The calculation is advisory. Amounts above front-line authority require human approval.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.35.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
