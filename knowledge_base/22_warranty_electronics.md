# Electronics Warranty

Many electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty for manufacturing faults. Batteries and consumable components may have different coverage. Proof of purchase or order lookup is required.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.22.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
