# Home Appliance Warranty

Selected home appliances have 24-month coverage where stated on the product page. Installation damage and misuse are excluded. Safety-related appliance faults should be escalated.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.23.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
