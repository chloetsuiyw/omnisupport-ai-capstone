# Potentially Lost Parcel

A parcel may be treated as potentially lost when tracking has not updated for 5 days after the expected delivery date. Agents should check carrier status before promising a refund or replacement.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.11.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
