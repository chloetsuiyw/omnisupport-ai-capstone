# Standard Returns Policy

Most unused items can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The item should be in resalable condition with original accessories where applicable. Fashion items may be tried on but should not show signs of extended wear. Return eligibility is assessed from the delivery date, not the order date. Marketplace-seller items follow the marketplace policy.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.01.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
