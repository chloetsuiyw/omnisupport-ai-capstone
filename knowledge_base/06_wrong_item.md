# Wrong Item Delivered

If the customer receives a materially different product, offer a no-cost return and replacement where stock is available. Do not require the customer to pay return postage. For low-value items, the agent may be authorised to waive physical return if the support tool indicates eligibility.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.06.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
