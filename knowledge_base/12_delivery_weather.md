# Weather Disruption

Severe weather can extend delivery estimates. Agents should not state that weather removes the customer's rights. Offer current status and options using the standard late-delivery policy.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.12.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
