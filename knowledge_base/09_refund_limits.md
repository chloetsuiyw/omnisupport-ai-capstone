# Refund Authority Limits

Front-line agents may recommend refunds up to GBP 100 when policy conditions are met. Refunds above GBP 100 require human approval from a senior agent. Refunds above GBP 500 must be escalated to the payments or risk team.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.09.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
