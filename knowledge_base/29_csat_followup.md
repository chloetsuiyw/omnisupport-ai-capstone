# Customer Satisfaction Follow-up

CSAT is collected after a case is closed. It must not be used as a feature for predictions made at ticket creation time unless the modelling task explicitly predicts a later outcome from data available after closure.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.29.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
