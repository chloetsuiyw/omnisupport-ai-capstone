# Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be appropriate for minor cosmetic damage when the customer wants to keep the item. The customer must agree to the amount. The system should not automatically issue a partial refund without confirmation.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.08.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
