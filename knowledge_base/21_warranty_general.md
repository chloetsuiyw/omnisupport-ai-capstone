# General Warranty Guidance

Warranty support applies to qualifying faults after the normal return window. Accidental damage is generally not a manufacturing fault. Warranty outcomes can include repair, replacement, or another remedy depending on product and age.

## Operational notes
- Scope: OmniSupport UK synthetic retail environment.
- Source: internal training policy snapshot.
- Version: 1.21.
- If the case falls outside this document, use the more specific policy or request human review.
